# Apex Product Strategy & Technology Roadmap (2026-2027)

## Section 1: Post-Quantum Cryptography Migration
In response to NIST quantum resilience guidelines, Apex engineering has initiated a phased migration to Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC). By Q2 2026, all TLS key exchanges will default to Kyber-768 (ML-KEM) and Dilithium digital signatures for intra-cluster traffic.

## Section 2: High-Performance Serverless Vector Indexing
Apex Cloud will launch a managed Serverless Vector Database service in Q1 2026, offering sub-10ms retrieval latency across 1 billion dense embeddings using HNSW (Hierarchical Navigable Small World) index graphs and IVF-PQ quantization.

## Section 3: Edge AI Acceleration & On-Device Models
By Q3 2026, Apex will introduce lightweight 3B parameter quantized models optimized for edge gateways and IoT hardware, enabling offline RAG retrieval without active cloud connectivity.
