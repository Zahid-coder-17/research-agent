# Corporate Environmental & Sustainability Strategy (2025-2035)

## Sustainability Commitments
Apex Global Technologies is committed to minimizing its ecological footprint through structured carbon reduction initiatives.

## Targets & Timelines
- Scope 1 & 2 Emissions: Apex targets a 60% reduction in direct operational (Scope 1) and purchased electricity (Scope 2) greenhouse gas emissions by the year 2030, compared to a 2020 baseline.
- Net-Zero Operations: Apex commits to achieving complete Net-Zero carbon emissions across all direct operations by the target year 2040.
- Renewable Energy Procurement: By 2028, 100% of electricity utilized by Apex data centers will be sourced from verified wind and solar power purchase agreements (PPAs).

## Waste & Resource Management
All hardware decommissioned from Apex data hubs undergoes certified hardware recycling. Single-use plastics have been eliminated from all corporate headquarters as of Q2.
*(Note: Scope 3 supply chain emission accounting guidelines remain under internal review and metrics have not been finalized or published).*
