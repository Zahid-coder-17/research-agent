# Corporate Environmental, Social & Sustainability Strategy (2025-2035)

## Section 1: Executive Environmental Roadmap
Apex Global Technologies is committed to driving decarbonization across all data center facilities, hardware supply chains, and administrative infrastructure over the next decade.

## Section 2: Greenhouse Gas Emission Targets & Timelines
- Scope 1 & Scope 2 Emissions: Apex targets a verified 60% reduction in direct operational (Scope 1) and purchased electricity (Scope 2) greenhouse gas emissions by the target year 2030, using 2020 baseline metrics.
- Net-Zero Operations Commitment: Apex commits to achieving complete Net-Zero carbon emissions across all direct operations by the target year 2040.
- Scope 3 Supply Chain Status: Scope 3 supply chain greenhouse gas emission accounting standards remain under internal review. Metrics and vendor procurement guidelines for Scope 3 have not been finalized or published.

## Section 3: Renewable Energy & Data Center Cooling
- Clean Energy Procurement: By 2028, 100% of electrical energy consumed by Apex cloud data centers will be backed by long-term renewable Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs) for solar and wind power.
- Water Usage Effectiveness (WUE): Flagship facilities in Frankfurt utilize direct evaporative cooling systems, achieving a WUE ratio of 1.15 liters per kWh.

## Section 4: Circular Economy & Electronic Waste
100% of decommissioned server chassis, solid-state drives, and network switches undergo certified e-waste recycling through ISO 14001 certified partners. Single-use plastics were eliminated from all corporate facilities as of Q2 2024.
