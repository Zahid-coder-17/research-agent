# Independent Auditor Report & Tax Reserve Footnotes (Q3)

## Section 1: Auditor Financial Disclosures
Independent accounting firm Deloitte & Touche LLP conducted a review of Apex Global Technologies' condensed financial statements for Q3.

## Section 2: Contingent Liabilities & Tax Reserves
Apex maintains a contingent tax reserve of $18.5 million regarding ongoing state sales tax audits in California and Texas. Management believes existing reserves are adequate to satisfy all potential assessment outcomes.

## Section 3: Revenue Amortization & Contract Assets
Audit procedures identified $42 million in unrecognized contract assets related to multi-year enterprise platform commitments. Amortization of these contract assets is scheduled across a 36-month linear schedule commencing Q1 of next fiscal year.
