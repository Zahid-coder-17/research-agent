# Apex Cloud Master Services Agreement & Terms of Service

## Section 1: Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Uptime Commitments
Apex Global Technologies guarantees a Monthly Uptime Percentage of 99.99% for Enterprise Tier cloud infrastructure. If uptime falls below 99.99% in a billing month, customers are eligible for Service Credits according to the following schedule:
- Uptime 99.90% to 99.98%: 10% Service Credit
- Uptime 99.00% to 99.89%: 25% Service Credit
- Uptime below 99.00%: 50% Service Credit

## Section 2: Data Ownership & Data Deletion Commitments
- Customer Data Ownership: Customers retain exclusive intellectual property ownership of all uploaded data, files, and fine-tuned model weights.
- Deletion SLA: Upon contract termination or explicit API deletion request, Apex warrants complete physical and logical purging of customer data from storage volumes and backup media within 30 business days.

## Section 3: Limitation of Liability & Indemnification
To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Apex's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to service interruptions shall not exceed the total fees paid by customer in the 12 months preceding the incident.
