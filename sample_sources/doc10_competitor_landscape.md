# Enterprise Cloud Infrastructure Competitor Analysis (2025)

## Section 1: Competitive Positioning & Market Dynamics
The enterprise cloud market remains dominated by three major hyperscalers: AWS (31% market share), Microsoft Azure (23% market share), and Google Cloud (11% market share). Apex Global Technologies holds a 14.2% specialized market share focused on developer AI pipelines.

## Section 2: Egress Pricing & Cost Comparisons
Apex offers zero egress bandwidth fees for intra-cloud transfers, compared to standard $0.09/GB egress pricing on rival hyperscalers. This pricing structure provides a 35% overall cost advantage for high-bandwidth LLM training workloads.
